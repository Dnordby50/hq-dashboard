Sidebar nav reorg - proposal

Overview is pinned at the top. Click a group header (Sales, Production, Finance, Admin) to open it; opening one closes the others, so the list never overflows. This file is a mockup only and is not wired into the live CRM.

Recommendation

Collapsible groups (single-open accordion), one bar, with Overview pinned. Overview (Dashboard, Metrics) is always visible since it is home. The other sections (Sales, Production, Finance, Admin) are toggles, and only one is open at a time, so the visible list stays short no matter how many views exist. The group holding your current view opens automatically, the selected item scrolls into view, and a collapsed group that contains the active view shows a small orange dot so you never lose your place.

Why this one for this codebase, not a flyout or a deep tree:

  • The groups already exist. #pecSubnav emits the section headers (.pec-subnav-group) and the sidebar already clones them in as labels. We are turning labels that are already there into toggles, not inventing a structure.
  • It stays in one rail, so it does not add a floating panel that has to manage its own positioning, outside-click close, Escape key, and z-index against the two modal roots and the bell panel (a known gotcha area in this app).
  • The active-state mirroring (index.html:4577 keeps the sidebar button in sync with switchView) keeps working untouched: the mirror buttons still exist in the DOM, they are just hidden inside a collapsed group, so the observer still toggles .active on them.

The options I compared

PatternCostTrade-off
Collapsible groups (recommended)LowBuilds on the existing clone + group headers. One bar, no overlay machinery. Single-open + auto-expand keeps the active view findable and never overflows.
Right-side flyoutHighBest for very deep nav, but adds a floating panel (positioning, outside-click, Escape, z-index vs the modal roots / bell) and a second active-state concept (which top-level group is lit). Overkill for ~17 items.
Inline dropdown / treeLow-MedFunctionally the same as collapsible groups at our depth (group, then views). Extra indentation buys nothing until views gain their own sub-views.
