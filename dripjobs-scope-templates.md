## Ameripolish Stain with Concrete Polishing System
Line item: Concrete Polishing with Stain

Project Overview:
This scope outlines the process and materials to be used in incorporating Ameripolish® SureLock™ Stain and ColorSolve™ into the concrete polishing system. The objective is to achieve a durable, vibrant, and consistent color finish or modeling look with long-term UV stability and stain resistance as part of the polished concrete process.

Work to be Performed:

**Surface Preparation**

Mechanically grind the concrete surface using progressive metal-bond diamond tooling to remove contaminants, laitance, and surface imperfections.

Ensure surface is clean, dry, and free from curing compounds, sealers, oils, and debris prior to application.

Vacuum and auto-scrub to ensure the slab is free of dust and residue.

**Densification (if applicable)**

Apply an approved lithium or sodium silicate densifier as specified in the polishing system.

Allow sufficient time for penetration and reaction prior to stain application.

**Color Application – Ameripolish® SureLock™ Stain**

Apply Ameripolish® SureLock™ Stain evenly using a low-pressure sprayer or microfiber applicator in accordance with manufacturer specifications.

Blend colors as needed to achieve desired tone and consistency.

Allow proper dry time per product data sheet before proceeding to next step.

**Solvent Carrier – Ameripolish® ColorSolve™**

Apply Ameripolish® ColorSolve™ as the approved carrier and stain enhancer for optimal color penetration and uniformity.

Ensure adequate ventilation during application and drying period.

**Polishing Progression**

Continue polishing process using progressively finer resin-bond diamond tooling up to the desired sheen level (matte, semi-gloss, or high-gloss).

Avoid over-polishing prior to color lock-in to maintain desired color depth.

**Protection / Stain Guard**

Apply Ameripolish® SR2 or approved surface guard for added protection and stain resistance.

Burnish with high-speed diamond pad to enhance clarity and seal.

**Final Cleanup and Inspection**

Remove all masking and protection materials.

Conduct final inspection with client to verify color consistency, sheen, and finish quality.

Provide maintenance recommendations for long-term care.

**Products to Be Used:**

Ameripolish® SureLock™ Stain (specified color(s))

Ameripolish® ColorSolve™ Carrier

Ameripolish® SR2 (if part of final protection system) OR, EZ Guard

Approved densifier and polishing diamonds per system specification

**Exclusions:**

Crack repair, joint filling, patching, or resurfacing outside the defined polishing area.

Moisture mitigation or vapor barrier installation.

Any substrate correction required due to pre-existing damage or contamination.

## Concrete Polishing without joint filler
Line item: Polishing without joint filler

SCOPE OF WORK -- Polished Concrete Flooring 800 Grit High-Polish Finish with Ameripolish Stain and Polyurea Joint Filling

Project: [Client Name] Address: [Street, City, AZ ZIP] Approximate SF: 3,000 Building Type: Dental Office (New Construction, Commercial)

**WORK AREA ACCESS**

Entire concrete surface must be 100% clear and ready for our team to begin upon arrival. This includes removal of all materials, equipment, and obstructions. Slab must be a minimum of 28 days cured prior to start. Adequate electrical service required on site (minimum two 20-amp 110V circuits and one 30-amp 220V circuit). Water supply available on site.

**WORK SEQUENCE**

Concrete polishing will be completed prior to painting to avoid overspray and surface contamination.

**POST-POLISHING PROTECTION**

Once our work is completed, floor covering and protection is the responsibility of the General Contractor (GC). We are not liable for damage caused by other trades after polishing is finished.

**SURFACE REPAIR AND PREPARATION**

Spall Repair: All spalled areas dime-sized or larger will be filled using concrete mender for improved durability and surface consistency.

Grout Coat (Optional): This scope does not include a grout coat for smaller pinholes or voids. If desired, this can be added as a change order.

**CONTROL JOINT FILLING**

All control joints and construction joints will be cleaned of debris and dust. Joints will be routed/chased to a uniform width and depth as needed to accept filler (minimum 1/4" wide x 1/4" deep). Joint edges cleaned with compressed air and/or vacuum to ensure proper adhesion.

Joints will be filled using a two-part semi-rigid polyurea joint filler designed for durability and movement tolerance. Filler applied via dual-cartridge dispensing system for proper mix ratio. Installed flush or slightly proud to allow shaving during the initial grinding pass. Cure time per manufacturer specs before grinding may proceed over filled joints.

Filler color selected by client. Standard options: gray, light gray, or custom color match if available from manufacturer.

This scope does not include filling of hairline cracks. If crack repair is needed, it can be completed as a change order upon request.

Note: Polyurea filler is semi-rigid and moves with the slab. It will be visible as a clean filled joint line in the finished floor. It is not intended to be invisible.

**POLISHING PROCESS (6-7 STEPS TO 800 GRIT)**

Step 1 -- 30/40 Grit (Metal Bond Diamond) Initial rough grind to open the slab, remove surface imperfections, and level high spots. Shave polyurea joint filler flush with slab surface. Full edge pass with hand grinder along all perimeter walls, corners, and obstructions.

Step 2 -- 80/100 Grit (Metal Bond Diamond) Remove scratches from previous grit. Continue leveling and refining surface profile. Full edge pass.

Step 3 -- 200 Grit (Transition: Metal or Resin Bond) Transition cut. Refine surface and prepare for densifier application. Full edge pass.

Densifier Application: High-performance concrete densifier applied to entire floor after 200-grit pass. Penetrates and chemically hardens surface, reduces porosity, improves abrasion resistance. Full cure per manufacturer specs before proceeding. Excess removed, no residue or haze.

Step 4 -- 400 Grit (Resin Bond Diamond) Begin honing phase. Surface develops noticeable sheen. Full edge pass.

Step 5 -- 800 Grit (Resin Bond Diamond) Final polishing pass. Produces a semi-gloss to high-gloss finish with strong light reflection and clarity. Full edge pass.

**AMERIPOLISH STAIN APPLICATION**

After completion of the grinding/polishing sequence and prior to final guard/sealer, Ameripolish dye stain will be applied to the full floor area.

Color(s) selected by client prior to mobilization. Sample/mock-up available upon request for color approval before full application.

Stain will be applied evenly across the entire surface using low-pressure sprayer and/or microfiber applicator per manufacturer specifications. Multiple coats applied as needed to achieve full, even color saturation. Edges and detail areas stained by hand to match field.

Excess stain residue will be removed. Floor will be buffed and prepped for guard/sealer application.

Note: Concrete stain is translucent and reacts with the surface. Final color appearance will vary based on concrete mix design, porosity, densifier absorption, and aggregate exposure. Color will not be perfectly uniform across the entire floor. Variations are inherent to the process and are not considered defects. We will make every effort to maintain consistency, but some variation is expected.

**SEALING AND BURNISHING**

Ameripolish SR2 oil-based guard sealer applied to protect against stains and wear. Sealer burnished for a clean, finished sheen consistent with the 800-grit polish level.

**AGGREGATE EXPOSURE**

Our standard polishing process will yield a "salt and pepper" aggregate exposure, where fine and medium sand-sized particles become visible through the surface. Inconsistencies may occur due to variations in the concrete's original finishing and mix design. We will make every effort to maintain uniformity, but some areas may expose more or less aggregate naturally depending on the slab's characteristics.

If a different exposure level is desired (cream finish or medium aggregate), this must be specified prior to start and may affect timeline and pricing.

**EXCLUSIONS**

Removal of existing flooring, coatings, adhesives, or sealers
Grout coat for pinholes/voids (available as change order)
Hairline crack repair (available as change order)
Leveling or patching of significantly uneven slabs
Decorative scoring or saw-cut patterns
Moisture mitigation systems (if testing reveals elevated levels, quoted separately)
Moving, removal, or reinstallation of fixtures or equipment
Floor protection after completion (GC responsibility)
Permitting

**ESTIMATED TIMELINE**

Timeline assumes unobstructed access to full floor area. Work phased around other trades will extend schedule and may affect pricing.

**CLIENT RESPONSIBILITIES**

Provide unobstructed access to all areas for duration of project
All other trades complete or protected prior to start
Slab minimum 28 days cured
Select joint filler color, stain color, and aggregate exposure level prior to mobilization
Designate point of contact available during work hours
Floor protection after completion is GC responsibility

## Packages Garage
Line item: Signature Full Flake Garage Floor

Our most popular system. Decorative vinyl flake broadcast over a high-performance epoxy base delivers depth, texture, and hide, masking concrete imperfections while standing up to daily abuse. Customizable colors and backed by our 10-year warranty.

**Day 1 - Epoxy Base + Flake Broadcast:** After surface preparation is complete, one coat of 100% solids epoxy is applied by squeegee and roller at approximately 10 mils film thickness. While the epoxy is still wet, decorative vinyl flakes are broadcast by hand to full rejection (meaning flake is applied until the surface cannot accept any more). This creates a seamless, fully covered finish. The garage door must remain open during application and for the first 24 hours of cure time. The floor cures overnight.

**Day 2 - Scrape, Prep + Polyaspartic Top Coat:** Excess flake is scraped and removed from the cured surface. The floor is thoroughly vacuumed. One coat of Simiron 98% solids clear gloss polyaspartic is applied by squeegee and roller, locking the flake in place and providing UV protection, chemical resistance, and a smooth, cleanable surface. Work area is cleaned and a final inspection is performed with you before we leave.

**Cure Times:** No foot traffic for 24 hours after final coat. No vehicles for 48 hours after final coat. Do not close the garage door for 24 hours after final coat.
